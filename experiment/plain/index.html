Systems

One stack. Three layers. Every aircraft.

The Atlas autonomy stack is modular by layer and integrated by design. Each layer runs onboard, fails independently, and degrades gracefully.

01

Axis — Autonomy Core

Full-stack flight autonomy: perception, localization, path planning, and adaptive control. Runs in a deterministic loop at 200 Hz on the vehicle, with zero dependence on external connectivity.

  • GPS-denied visual-inertial odometry
  • Mission planning to 400 waypoints, re-planned in 90 ms
  • Certifiable failure modes, human-takeover < 300 ms

02

Vireo — Sensing Suite

A fused payload of EO/IR imaging, 360° radar, and LiDAR in a 1.9 kg gimbal. One data bus, one power feed, one timebase. Every frame timestamped to 1 ms.

  • Detects fixed vehicles at 8.2 km, moving at 5.4 km
  • All-weather tracking through rain and smoke
  • Onboard classification of 1,100 object classes

03

Nexus — Ground Operator

A single operator console for fleets, not aircraft. Task-level command, live sensor feeds, and health telemetry over low-bandwidth links — including a 2.4 kbps degraded mode.

  • One operator supervises 12 aircraft
  • Open API; integrates with existing C2 systems
  • Full mission replay with sensor-fusion timeline
