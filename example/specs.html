Where A-1 is used

Long-endurance overwatch for maritime corridors and land borders: persistent sensing with a 4.8 kg multi-mission payload bay, launching from rail ramps on vessels or trailer rails on land.

Because airframes are priced to be lost, attrition is part of the operating concept — recovery is desired, never required. A single launch crew can cycle 12 airframes per day.

Built for the fleet of the next decade.
